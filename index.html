▶ Play now How it plays

Four modes

A coach mode that reduces the first trade to one question, then standard, hard, and contract runs with their own endings.

Fourteen crisis files

Historical crashes, rewritten as case files to read between trades.

Saves on your device

Progress lives in the browser. Nothing is uploaded, and nobody is watching.
